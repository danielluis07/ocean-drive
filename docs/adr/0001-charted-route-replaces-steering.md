# Charted Route replaces visitor steering

The Expedition originally used a Guided Helm: the Research Vessel moved at a steady pace while the Visitor steered its heading, supported by Environmental Wayfinding, Assisted Return, gated Field Stations, and a Visitor-chosen middle-station order. That model required persistent helm controls, guidance text, and recovery messaging, which made the screen noisy and instruction-heavy. We replaced it with a Charted Route: scrolling (or keys, or the chapters menu) sails the vessel along one fixed, ordered route, settling on the nearest Field Station, with no gating and no confirmation buttons. This trades the Visitor's sense of agency for a clean, near-instruction-free presentation modelled on scroll-driven editorial experiences.

## Considered Options

- Keep steering and hide most guidance — rejected: the instructions exist because steering needs them.
- Scroll-driven with optional steering — rejected: keeps the complexity of both models.
